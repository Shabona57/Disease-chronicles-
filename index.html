Insoniyat kasalliklari arxivi An archive of human illness

La'natdan davolanishgacha — har bir kasallik o'z tarixi bilan. From curse to cure, one disease at a time.

Har bir kasallikning o'z tarjimai holi bor — birinchi marta kashf etilgan payt, asrlar davomida noto'g'ri taxminlar va ilmning asta-sekin haqiqatga yetib borishi. Bu yerda o'sha tarixlar saqlanadi. Every disease has a biography — a moment of first discovery, centuries of wrong guesses, and the slow work of science getting it right. This is where those stories are filed.
